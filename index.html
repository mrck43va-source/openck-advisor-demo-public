Portfolio pitch

OpenCK is building businesses in stages: concept → demo → V1 → first customer → repeatable revenue → scale. Current businesses are used as proof-of-execution before expanding into additional online, AI, cyber, physical, and brick-and-mortar businesses. Revenue, reserves, and later outside capital fund the next layers rather than launching everything simultaneously.
