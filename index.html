Portfolio pitch

OpenCK is building businesses in stages: concept → demo → V1 → first customer → repeatable revenue → scale. Current businesses are used as proof-of-execution before expanding into additional online, AI, cyber, physical, and brick-and-mortar businesses. Revenue, reserves, and later outside capital fund the next layers rather than launching everything simultaneously.

Contact / Advisor Follow-Up

What we want advisors to challenge

Revenue priority, pricing and positioning, customer acquisition, Lead Automation commercial structure, Content Studio pilot economics, e-commerce supplier/product validation, legal/CPA/insurance referrals, and the 30/60/90-day operating plan.

Status language is evidence-based: DEMO PASS does not equal Commercial GO. Synthetic activity, trading results, projections, pipeline values, and uncollected amounts are not presented as realized business revenue.
